What the brief reads

Sources

The Monday run checks these outlets, then keeps only the pieces that actually moved in the United States. A story about another country stays out, even when a U.S. outlet wrote it. Competitor updates, show agendas, and app-store reviews are folded into New tech and School rather than left as a separate pile.

New tech

Kidscreen

Trade site · Daily

Kids media and product news, including safety, privacy, and what is about to ship.

EdSurge

News site · Daily

Product launches and district adoption, mostly school software.

Wareable

Reviews · Weekly

Wearable launches. Only when the product is sold in the United States.

Wirecutter

Buyer’s guides · When updated

The guide parents trust when they search for a kids smartwatch.

Competitor release notes

Product changelogs · Monthly

Xplora, Gabb, Bark, Pinwheel, TickTalk, Garmin Bounce, and Apple Watch Family Setup. Ship notes and app-store version history.

App store reviews

Parent complaints · Monthly

One-star reviews on kids-watch apps: GPS, battery, school hours, and subscription anger.

CES and Toy Fair

Trade shows · January and February

Where new kids hardware is shown. The agenda is the signal.

Edutopia

ISTE and SXSW EDU

Conferences · Annual

What teachers will sit through a session to hear.

School board phone policies

Local policy · Ongoing

Statewide phone bans and district policies. These change what a school mode has to do.

Workshops

Companies that teach children STEM, or run classes, camps, and after-school sessions. The point is a partner who already has the child in the room, doing the work, where a companion can sit beside that session. What each program has kids do is on the Programs page.

Code Ninjas, theCoderSchool, and Snapology

STEM operators · When they publish

Coding, robotics, and build sessions, usually after school or on weekends. A new program, a curriculum change, or a partnership is the piece. One local class listing is not.

iD Tech and Bricks 4 Kidz

Camps and workshops · When they publish

Multi-day camps and in-school workshops. Useful when they add a subject, an age band, or a school partnership.

FIRST and Camp Invention

National programs · When they publish

FIRST LEGO League and the National Inventors Hall of Fame’s Camp Invention. Seasonal programs with a shared curriculum, which is an easier companion fit than a one-off party.

Girls Who Code

Clubs · When they publish

After-school clubs with a set curriculum. A companion has to fit the club, not replace the teacher.

Mathnasium, Sylvan, Kumon, and Huntington

Tutoring · When they publish

Practice sessions rather than build workshops. Same collaboration idea: the child is already sitting down to do the work.

Afterschool Alliance

Association · Monthly

Who funds and runs after-school time in the United States. The map of rooms a companion could enter.

HolonIQ

Research notes · Monthly

The funding scoreboard. Keep the United States slice. A round in another country stays out.

Reach Capital

Owl Ventures

Crunchbase News

Round announcements for U.S. companies. Read with a saved search for edtech, kids, and family.

BCG education

Consulting · When published

Deloitte Insights

Consulting surveys · Annual

Whiteboard Advisors

Bellwether

Joan Ganz Cooney Center

Sesame Workshop’s shop for kids and digital media. Closer to child development than to software.

Brookings education

Think tank · Monthly

School quality, screens, and policy.

Circana

Retail research · Quarterly

Toy and juvenile sell-through. Press releases are free. The workbook is paid.

Occasional, not a standing read

Accenture, PwC, EY, KPMG, and Bain publish education work that is often higher education or workforce. Read a piece when it is actually about school or families. Gartner and Forrester rarely beat HolonIQ, Tyton, or Circana on kids hardware.

Rules and trust

FTC

Regulator · When it acts

COPPA updates and enforcement. Precise location, voice, and biometrics are personal information under the amended rule.

CARU / BBB National Programs

Self-regulatory · When it acts

Children’s advertising and privacy cases.

Fairplay

Advocacy · Monthly

The organized U.S. critique of commercial kids tech. Their objections preview press cycles.

Education Commission of the States

Policy tracker · Ongoing

State legislation on devices, AI, and school safety.

AAP policy

Clinical guidance · When published

Pediatricians shape what parents believe is safe.
