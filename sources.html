What the brief reads

Sources

The Monday run checks these outlets, then keeps only the pieces that actually moved. Competitor updates, show agendas, and app-store reviews are folded into New tech and School rather than left as a separate pile.

New tech

Kidscreen

Trade site · Daily

Kids media and product news, including safety, privacy, and what is about to ship.

EdSurge

News site · Daily

Product launches and district adoption, mostly school software.

Wareable

Reviews · Weekly

Wearable launches. Kids watches show up next to Garmin and Apple.

Wirecutter

Buyer’s guides · When updated

The guide parents trust when they search for a kids smartwatch.

Competitor release notes

Product changelogs · Monthly

Xplora, Gabb, Bark, Pinwheel, TickTalk, Garmin Bounce, and Apple Watch Family Setup. Ship notes and app-store version history.

App store reviews

Parent complaints · Monthly

One-star reviews on kids-watch apps: GPS, battery, school hours, and subscription anger.

CES and Toy Fair

Trade shows · January and February

Where new kids hardware is shown. The agenda is the signal.

Parents and teachers

After Babel

Newsletter · Weekly

Jonathan Haidt. Sets the parent and school conversation on phones, anxiety, and bans.

ParentData

Newsletter and podcast · Weekly

Emily Oster. Evidence-minded parents who ask for data before they buy a safety device.

Cult of Pedagogy

Blog and podcast · Monthly

Jennifer Gonzalez writing to working teachers.

Edutopia

Teacher site · Weekly

Teacher-written practice on attention, belonging, and tools.

Reddit: r/Teachers, r/Parenting, r/edtech

Community · Ongoing

Unpolished complaints. Search smartwatch, phone ban, and school AI.

Understood.org

Parent site · Ongoing

Learning and attention differences. A large share of watch buyers are here.

Child Mind Institute

Clinical explainers · Ongoing

How clinicians explain screens, anxiety, and school refusal to parents.

National PTA

Association · Monthly

Official parent voice, plus the Notes from the Backpack podcast.

PDK Poll

Annual poll

What the public thinks of public schools.

School

The 74

News site · Daily

School policy, phones, absenteeism, and parent politics.

Education Week

News site · Daily

What teachers and district leaders are arguing about, including AI rules and devices.

K-12 Dive

Trade news · Daily

Short briefs on district buying, AI, and operations.

Chalkbeat

Local news · Daily

Classroom reality in specific cities: staffing, phones, safety, and parent anger.

The Hechinger Report

News site · Weekly

Longer investigations on learning, inequality, and technology that failed its promise.

ISTE and SXSW EDU

Conferences · Annual

What teachers will sit through a session to hear.

School board phone policies

Local policy · Ongoing

Statewide phone bans and district policies. These change what a school mode has to do.

Investments

Edtech Insiders

Newsletter and podcast · Weekly

Deal flow, founder interviews, and the K-12 AI market maps investors use.

HolonIQ

Research notes · Monthly

The funding scoreboard for education venture and growth capital.

EdWeek Market Brief

Paid trade · Weekly

How districts buy. More useful once a product sells into schools.

Reach Capital

Investor essays · Monthly

An edtech fund that writes its thesis in public.

ASU+GSV and GSV

Summit and fund · Annual

The main education-investing gathering. The agenda is an early map of what capital cares about.

Owl Ventures

Investor notes · When published

A large education-only fund. Portfolio and memos show where institutional money sits.

Brighteye Ventures

European fund notes · Monthly

Useful because kids-safety rules in Europe often arrive before U.S. copies.

Crunchbase News

Deal news · Daily

Round announcements. Read with a saved search for edtech, kids, and family.

Research

Tyton Partners

Consulting notes · Quarterly

Education specialist bank. Free deal recaps and segment reads.

Common Sense Media

Research and ratings · When published

Parent surveys and product verdicts, including AI toys and companions.

RAND education surveys

Survey panels · When published

National samples of teachers, principals, students, and parents.

Pew Research Center

Surveys · Annual

Teens, parents, and tech numbers that reporters and policymakers quote.

Project Tomorrow Speak Up

Annual survey

Students, parents, and teachers on school technology, in one instrument.

McKinsey Education

Consulting · When published

System-level K-12 work. Skeptical of handing every child a device.

BCG education

Consulting · When published

Recent work is heavy on AI and skills, more system than kids hardware.

Deloitte Insights

Consulting surveys · Annual

Back-to-school parent spending, plus consumer tech. A retail read.

Whiteboard Advisors

Policy consulting · Monthly

What districts and agencies are about to ask vendors.

Bellwether

Education consultancy · Monthly

Independent K-12 analysis used by funders and operators.

Joan Ganz Cooney Center

Research center · When published

Sesame Workshop’s shop for kids and digital media. Closer to child development than to software.

Brookings education

Think tank · Monthly

School quality, screens, and policy.

Circana

Retail research · Quarterly

Toy and juvenile sell-through. Press releases are free. The workbook is paid.

Occasional, not a standing read

Accenture, PwC, EY, KPMG, and Bain publish education work that is often higher education or workforce. Read a piece when it is actually about school or families. Gartner and Forrester rarely beat HolonIQ, Tyton, or Circana on kids hardware.

Rules and trust

FTC

Regulator · When it acts

COPPA updates and enforcement. Precise location, voice, and biometrics are personal information under the amended rule.

CARU / BBB National Programs

Self-regulatory · When it acts

Children’s advertising and privacy cases.

5Rights Foundation

Advocacy · Monthly

Age-appropriate design, the frame behind the UK children’s code and a lot of European kids policy.

Fairplay

Advocacy · Monthly

The organized U.S. critique of commercial kids tech. Their objections preview press cycles.

UK ICO Children’s Code

Regulator · When it acts

Age-appropriate design. Product choices that pass here travel.

Australia eSafety Commissioner

Regulator · When it acts

A strict youth-safety regime, including under-16 social media limits.

Education Commission of the States

Policy tracker · Ongoing

State legislation on devices, AI, and school safety.

AAP policy

Clinical guidance · When published

Pediatricians shape what parents believe is safe.

Follow the outlet, not every post. The brief keeps the pieces that change the week.
